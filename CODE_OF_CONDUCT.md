# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in AntiClaude a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by mistakes, and learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Sexualized language or imagery, sexual attention, or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project in public spaces. Project spaces include the repository, issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project communication channels.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the project maintainer, [@pxxguin](https://github.com/pxxguin), using the contact options available on the maintainer's GitHub profile. If the conduct occurs on GitHub and private contact is unavailable or inappropriate, use [GitHub's reporting tools](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).

All reports will be reviewed and investigated fairly. The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. The privacy and safety of reporters will be respected to the extent reasonably possible.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Corrective action will be proportional to the nature, impact, and persistence of the behavior. It may include a private warning, removal of content, temporary exclusion, or a permanent ban from project interaction.
